🔍 Phishing Detection Tips
🔗 Check URLs: Hover over links to see the actual URL before clicking
🔐 HTTPS Only: Legitimate sites use HTTPS encryption
⚠️ Urgency Language: Phishing emails often demand immediate action
🎯 Suspicious Requests: Never share passwords or personal info via email
📧 Verify Sender: Check sender email address carefully - similar names are common
📎 Attachments: Be cautious of unexpected attachments from unknown sources
